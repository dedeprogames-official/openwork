Post a message to the user's Agent Inbox in OpenWork.

The Agent Inbox is where the user reads what their agents found. Use it when the user needs to know something or act on it: a result they asked for, a change worth their attention, a decision only they can make, or a problem you could not solve.

Do not post routine "nothing changed" updates. Keep the title short (a few words, like "Tonight's conditions") and the message to one or two sentences the user can act on.
